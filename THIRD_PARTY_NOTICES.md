## Berean Standard Bible

Suggested reading-unit boundaries in `app/bible-structure.ts` are derived from section, paragraph, and stanza markers in the [Berean Standard Bible v5.9 USFM release](https://github.com/BSB-publishing/bsb2usfm/releases/tag/v5.9). The Berean Standard Bible was dedicated to the public domain on April 30, 2023. No Berean Standard Bible verse text is included in this project; only numeric reading-unit boundaries are retained.
